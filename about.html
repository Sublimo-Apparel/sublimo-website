Our story

Back to
step one.

Sublimo wasn't our first try. Before it, we ran another business, and it ended in a loss. Just when it felt like we were getting somewhere, we were back at step one.

We started again anyway. Part of what kept us going was simple: we didn't want the people working with us to lose their jobs.

That second start became Sublimo, a full-sublimation shop in Tabaco City, Albay. Our layout artists design every order, and we print, cut and sew each piece in our own shop. Today, more than 500 teams wear Sublimo, and their photos cover the wall we call Framed in Fabric.
