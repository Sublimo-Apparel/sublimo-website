How to order

Three steps. That's it.

From first message to team photo. Here's how we work, what rush costs, and how to find the right size.

Steps Fees & payment Size chart Contact
01

Design it.

  • Message us on Messenger with your idea, logo, colors and quantity.
  • We make the layout. Free for 10 pcs and up, ₱500 for less than 10 pcs.
  • Approve the mockup and send names, numbers and sizes.
02

Print it.

  • Pay a 50% downpayment to start production.
  • Standard orders take 5–7 days, bulk orders 10–21 days, depending on our production load.
  • Need it sooner? Rush orders are ready in 1–4 days.
03

Wear it.

  • Pay the remaining 50% before release.
  • Pick up at our shop, get free delivery in Tabaco City, or ship nationwide via J&T.
  • Take a team photo and tag us.
